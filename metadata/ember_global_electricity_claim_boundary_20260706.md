# Claim boundary: Ember global electricity benchmark

This Ember layer supports annual country-level electricity-demand, total-generation, net-import and power-sector CO2-intensity benchmarking only. It is appropriate for Supplementary Information benchmarks, manuscript-safe annual electricity/carbon context, and coverage audit of annual host-country benchmark parameters. It does not provide hourly marginal emissions, facility-level electricity consumption, provider procurement, AI-specific dispatch, observed routing, or market-based Scope 2 accounting.
